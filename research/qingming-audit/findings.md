# Findings

The project has 13 stories; several combine observable detail with inferred identity, exact counts and modern comparisons. Source files contain image provenance but no per-story citations. Existing SOURCE.md is prior research, to be verified independently.

## First five image checks
- taxi: veiled rider and heavily loaded single-wheel cart visible; age, sex, hire status and luggage ownership cannot be established from this crop.
- relay: seated/reclining men, boxes and long weapons visible; exact nine and uniformed identity require stronger support.
- farewell: saddled animal and people gesturing/bowing visible; farewell is an interpretation; country/end-of-scroll claim is wrong for this location (city-side segment).
- ten-thousand: visible sign supports 脚店 and 十千 shop reading with wider context; bamboo material, licence and pricing interpretation need evidence.
- delivery: man holds several vessels on one hand and a handled rectangular carrier in the other; exactly two bowls is not supported. No proof of destination.

Primary leads found: Palace Museum object 228226; Yu Hui lecture 99722 (interpretive argument, not neutral certainty); dispute paper 100185 (huabiao versus wind vane); Palace journal PDF u5ebbc55aba64c (huanmen). Museum object initial fetch timed out; search text available, retry via focused open.

## Bridge and towpath image checks
- right-of-way: mounted rider and covered chair/palanquin visible, gesturing figures between. Crop is bridge approach/slope, not unambiguously crest. Military/civil identity and dispute require attribution.
- poles: bird-shaped finial and crosspieces visible on selected pole. Function disputed in museum scholarship; retain explicit uncertainty.
- close-call: large cargo boat, long diagonal spar, multiple active crew and bridge onlookers. Already diagonally lowered mast, not upright; collision has not visibly happened. Current, shouts, rope-throwing and warning are interpretations, not directly certain.
- towpath: five rope-hauling people visually countable. Shoreline and long line visible. 'Upstream of bridge', cargo, miles and precise current direction unsupported at hotspot scale.

## Last four image checks
- rudder: selected detail is a stern assembly with a broad decorated vertical component and long horizontal projecting timber; no operator visible in this crop. Current categorical sweep/oar identification is unsafe and title conflicts with internal id. Need technical evidence or neutral wording.
- oar: group clustered around a long timber on a vessel obscured partly by willow; exact eight, standing still, and common-oar steering not established by this image alone.
- table: raised open-sided waterside building, tables and seated figures directly visible; 'upstairs' misleading (no clear lower storey); time of dinner unknowable.
- life-aboard: people on cabin roof and in an opening, wicker/bundled items visible; bedding, permanent home, cooking and family identity not established.
