# Gap and duplicate review

Based on the first proposal's 22 accepted markers. Since then the mandarin duck (in the gate-to-peacocks stretch) and the fan runner (in the portrait-to-signature stretch) were added, which shortens the two longest gaps to about 3,700 px and 4,800 px. Painted silk is about 48,300 px wide, roughly 84 px per cm. Numbers are distances between neighbouring markers, right to left.
