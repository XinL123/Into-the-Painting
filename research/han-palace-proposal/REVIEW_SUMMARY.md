## What changed in this revision

1. **Cat:** kept, retitled "Who Is Napping Up There?". The card no longer says it is a cat; it asks "A cat? A lapdog?" and gives the catalogue's listing as the best guess.
2. **Portrait:** both readings kept. The doubt is now attributed to scholars in general (Xu Wenmei reviews Miyazaki Noriko 2005 and Qin Xiaolei), and the card adds that the museum's own notes say the theme has no single fixed story.
3. **Added:** "A Fan Raised High" (gesture only, no butterfly claim) and "A Duck on the Railing?" (bird re-checked at native size: a drake mandarin duck). The other three stay optional.
4. **Stacked pairs:** all kept. Measured in the running app on a 375 px wide phone, the closest two markers are 188 px apart (embroidery/ironing), then 221 px (ensemble/the napper). Nothing overlaps and nothing needed moving.
5. **Colour:** matched to the museum's own photographs; see `MASTER_SOURCE.md`.
6. **Extent:** the full scan is kept, seal margins included (about 4% of the width at each end).

**Edition:** confirmed again. The museum photographs used for colour carry the label 故畫001038 and register feature-for-feature onto this master.
